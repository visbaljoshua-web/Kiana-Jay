Behind the Lens

How the story began

It started, as the best stories do, with something small — a glance held a second too long, a coffee order remembered without being asked. From there, everything else followed quietly, the way morning light moves across a room.

Every frame in this collection was chosen to hold a little of that quiet: the in-between moments, the laughter caught off-guard, the stillness right before "I do." This is less an album than a keepsake — something to return to, long after the day itself has become memory.
